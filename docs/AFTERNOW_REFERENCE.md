# AFTERNOW REFERENCE & DESIGN EXTRACTION

> **Benchmark Reference**: [https://afternow.co/](https://afternow.co/)  
> **Target Brand**: **MUHAB — Saudi Webmakers** (Digital Solutions & Creative Services)

---

## 1. Visual Hierarchy & Page Rhythm

- **Oversized Editorial Typography**: Section headlines occupy 8–9 columns on a 12-column grid, creating immediate authority and tension against a 3-column action or metadata block.
- **Generous Vertical Spacing**: Section paddings are expansive (120px–180px on desktop, 64px–96px on mobile), giving every message space to breathe.
- **Asymmetrical Compositions**: Avoiding monotonous centered boxes; pairing left-aligned bold statements with right-aligned action buttons and sub-descriptions.
- **Minimalist Dividers**: Fine 1px subtle hairline rules (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` on dark, `rgba(0,0,0,0.08)` on light) structure the content without feeling cluttered.

---

## 2. Project Presentation & Work Showcase

- **Cinematic Media Framing**: Project media containers feature generous proportions, 16–24px smooth corner radii, subtle dark-tinted backgrounds, and high-framerate video/image previews.
- **Structured Storytelling Metadata**:
  - Category / Service Badges (e.g., *Strategy*, *Brand*, *Website*, *Development*)
  - Client Name in confident display font
  - One-sentence impact thesis (*"Making AI tangible for enterprises navigating transformation"*)
  - Measurable / Qualitative Result pill (*"Partners since 2024"*, *"27% Lift in conversion"*, *"300+ unique assets"*)
- **Hover Micro-Interactions**: Smooth scale (1.02–1.04x) with clip-path containment and cursor-following action labels.

---

## 3. Motion Language & Choreography

- **Masked Text Overflow Reveals**: Hero headlines and major section titles rise from behind `overflow: hidden` containers (`translateY(100%) → translateY(0%)`) with a high-end cubic bezier ease `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.
- **Staggered Sequences**: Eyebrow (100ms) → Headline lines (250–750ms) → Supporting copy (550–850ms) → CTA & Hero Visual (700–1100ms).
- **Controlled Parallax**: Subtle 0.05–0.15 scroll multipliers for background geometric elements and inner project images.
- **Client Logo Marquee**: Deliberately slow, steady infinite auto-scroll (`80s` animation duration) maintaining high dignity.

---

## 4. Navigation & Layout Continuity

- **Header Behavior**: Transparent on hero, morphs into a frosted glass pill/bar on scroll with crisp contrast against dark/light sections.
- **Fullscreen Navigation**: Expansive modal with quick case-study highlights, oversized navigation links, social channels, and direct inquiry CTA.
- **Footer Impact**: Closes with an oversized conversational statement (*"Have a business worth noticing? Let's build the digital presence it deserves"*), a primary magnet CTA, and full agency directory.
